# Grave Mode

**Ἦχος Βαρύς** · Tone 7 in the Sunday cycle

| Base | Genus | Intonation (apechema) |
|---|---|---|
| Ga or low Zo | enharmonic (on Ga) / diatonic (on low Zo) | Ἀανές (Aanes) |

Grave Mode in the workbook sits on Ga with an enharmonic lower Zo. Other repertoire places it on low Zo in the diatonic genus. Always read the opening martyria.

## Drone and scale

Scale from Ga in this vault's model (enharmonic, approximate), in moria:

`Ga (12)  Di (12)  Ke (6)  Zo (12)  Ni' (12)  Pa' (12)  Vou' (6)  Ga'`

```psaltiki-drone
genus: enharmonic
base: Ga
```
