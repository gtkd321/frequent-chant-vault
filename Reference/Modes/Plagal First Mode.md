# Plagal First Mode

**Ἦχος Πλάγιος τοῦ Πρώτου** · Tone 5 in the Sunday cycle

| Base | Genus | Intonation (apechema) |
|---|---|---|
| Pa (low Ke in some melodies) | diatonic | Ἀνεανές (Aneanes) |

Plagal First is the plagal of the First Mode on Pa. Many melodies descend to low Ke. Listen for cadences on Pa and the pull of Di above.

## Drone and scale

Scale from Pa in this vault's model (diatonic), in moria:

`Pa (10)  Vou (8)  Ga (12)  Di (12)  Ke (10)  Zo (8)  Ni' (12)  Pa'`

```psaltiki-drone
genus: diatonic
base: Pa
```
