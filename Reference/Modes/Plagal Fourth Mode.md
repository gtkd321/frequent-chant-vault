# Plagal Fourth Mode

**Ἦχος Πλάγιος τοῦ Τετάρτου** · Tone 8 in the Sunday cycle

| Base | Genus | Intonation (apechema) |
|---|---|---|
| Ni | diatonic | Νεάγιε (Neagie) |

Plagal Fourth sits on Ni in the diatonic genus, the scale you drilled in Block 1. Heirmologic Plagal Fourth often cadences on Ga, and its sticheraric melodies on Ni.

## Drone and scale

Scale from Ni in this vault's model (diatonic), in moria:

`Ni (12)  Pa (10)  Vou (8)  Ga (12)  Di (12)  Ke (10)  Zo (8)  Ni'`

```psaltiki-drone
genus: diatonic
base: Ni
```
