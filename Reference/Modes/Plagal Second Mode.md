# Plagal Second Mode

**Ἦχος Πλάγιος τοῦ Δευτέρου** · Tone 6 in the Sunday cycle

| Base | Genus | Intonation (apechema) |
|---|---|---|
| Pa | hard chromatic | Νεχεανές (Necheanes) |

Plagal Second uses the hard chromatic genus on Pa. The big Vou–Ga step is the signature sound. Sing it slowly against the drone until it feels natural.

## Drone and scale

Scale from Pa in this vault's model (hard chromatic), in moria:

`Pa (6)  Vou (20)  Ga (4)  Di (12)  Ke (6)  Zo (20)  Ni' (4)  Pa'`

```psaltiki-drone
genus: hard
base: Pa
```
