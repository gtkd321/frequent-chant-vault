# Third Mode

**Ἦχος Τρίτος** · Tone 3 in the Sunday cycle

| Base | Genus | Intonation (apechema) |
|---|---|---|
| Ga | enharmonic | Νανά (Nana) |

Third Mode sits on Ga. The Nana tetrachord gives it a bright, "major" sound with a lowered Zo. Ga, Di and Ni' are its pillars.

## Drone and scale

Scale from Ga in this vault's model (enharmonic, approximate), in moria:

`Ga (12)  Di (12)  Ke (6)  Zo (12)  Ni' (12)  Pa' (12)  Vou' (6)  Ga'`

```psaltiki-drone
genus: enharmonic
base: Ga
```
