# Second Mode

**Ἦχος Δεύτερος** · Tone 2 in the Sunday cycle

| Base | Genus | Intonation (apechema) |
|---|---|---|
| Di | soft chromatic | Νεανές (Neanes) |

Second Mode sits on Di in the soft chromatic genus. Listen for the narrow Di–Ke step and the wide Ke–Zo step. Its fast melodies often borrow a hard-chromatic color, so check with your teacher how your choir sings them.

## Drone and scale

Scale from Di in this vault's model (soft chromatic), in moria:

`Di (8)  Ke (14)  Zo (8)  Ni' (8)  Pa' (14)  Vou' (8)  Ga' (12)  Di'`

```psaltiki-drone
genus: soft
base: Di
```
