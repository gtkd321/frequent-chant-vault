# Sources

- **Workbook:** Konstantinos Margaziotis, Μελωδικαὶ Ἀσκήσεις (1968), with embedded recordings, published by St Anthony's Greek Orthodox Monastery: [Margaziotis.pdf](https://music.samonastery.org/Margaziotis.pdf). The pages and audio in this vault are copied from that PDF for offline use, for personal study only.
- **St Anthony's Monastery music library:** [Byzantine music links](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/byzantine-music-links) · [Orthros](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/orthros-byzantine-notation) · [Vespers](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/vespers-byzantine-notation) · [Divine Liturgy](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/divine-liturgy-byzantine-notation-modern) · [Formulae](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/byzantine-music-formulae) · [Papadic formulae](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/papadic-formulae) · [Divine Music Project](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/the-divine-music-project)
- **Glossary and workshop:** [Glossary](https://music.samonastery.org/Glossary.pdf) · [Formulae workshop](https://music.samonastery.org/Workshop.pdf)
- **Unseen scores for cold reading:** [GOA Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html)
- **Write your own exercises:** [Neanes scorewriter](https://neanes.github.io/neanes/)
- **Theory reference:** [Unicode Technical Note 20](http://www.unicode.org/notes/tn20/byznotation.pdf)
