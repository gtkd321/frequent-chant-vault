# Genera and Scales

Byzantine theory, as set out by the Patriarchal Music Committee of 1881, divides the octave into **72 moria**. A genus (γένος) is a pattern of steps. This vault uses these values, and the drone, ear quiz and tuner use the same numbers.

## Diatonic (Ni-based)

`Ni (12)  Pa (10)  Vou (8)  Ga (12)  Di (12)  Ke (10)  Zo (8)  Ni'`

Used by First, Fourth, Plagal First and Plagal Fourth Modes, and by the Grave on low Zo.

## Soft chromatic (Second Mode, on Di)

`Di (8)  Ke (14)  Zo (8)  Ni' (8)  Pa' (14)  Vou' (8)  Ga' (12)  Di'`

Built from Ni: `Ni (8)  Pa (14)  Vou (8)  Ga (12)  Di (8)  Ke (14)  Zo (8)  Ni'`

## Hard chromatic (Plagal Second, on Pa)

`Pa (6)  Vou (20)  Ga (4)  Di (12)  Ke (6)  Zo (20)  Ni' (4)  Pa'`

## Enharmonic (Third Mode and Grave, on Ga), approximate

`Ga (12)  Di (12)  Ke (6)  Zo (12)  Ni' (12)  Pa' (12)  Vou' (6)  Ga'`

Schools describe the enharmonic differently. Some speak of a lowered Zo inside a diatonic frame, and others of a true enharmonic tetrachord with a very small step. This vault uses an approximation, so ask your teacher how your tradition sings it and trust your ear and your teacher over the numbers.

## Pitch

The plugin plays Ni at 130.81 Hz by default (a low C). Change it under Settings → Frequent Chant to suit your voice or your choir.

## Try it

```psaltiki-drone
genus: diatonic
base: Ni
```
