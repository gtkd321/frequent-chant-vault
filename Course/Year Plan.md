- **Block 4 · All eight modes, two tempos**: weeks 22–37 (days 127–222)
- **Block 5 · Modulation and alteration**: weeks 38–42 (days 223–252)
- **Block 6 · Papadic tempo**: weeks 43–45 (days 253–270)
- **Block 7 · Capstone: read any score**: weeks 46–48 (days 271–288)
