# Week 44 · Papadic reading II

Block 6 · Papadic tempo

**Goal:** Communion hymns (koinonika), and the papadic formulae of the remaining modes.

## Days

### Day 259 (Monday)

[Second Mode papadic](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula2p.pdf) and [Second Mode hard-chromatic papadic](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula2ph.pdf): sing 3 from each.

### Day 260 (Tuesday)

[Third Mode papadic](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula3p.pdf) and [Grave papadic](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula7pp.pdf): sing 3 from each.

### Day 261 (Wednesday)

[Plagal First papadic](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula5p.pdf) and [Plagal Second papadic](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula6p.pdf): sing 3 from each.

### Day 262 (Thursday)

From the [Divine Liturgy scores](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/divine-liturgy-byzantine-notation-modern), read a Sunday koinonikon (Αἰνεῖτε): first page.

### Day 263 (Friday)

Same koinonikon, next page.

### Day 264 (Saturday)

Self-check: finish the Cherubic Hymn you started last week. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
