# Week 38 · Phthorae in practice

Block 5 · Modulation and alteration

**Goal:** Track the scale through every phthora without losing your place.

## Days

### Day 223 (Monday)

Reread [[Phthorae and Chroai]]. Make a card in My Work for each phthora you meet this week.

### Day 224 (Tuesday)

Read the [Third Mode Doxastikon (Glory)](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2330_Glory.pdf) and circle each phthora before you sing.

### Day 225 (Wednesday)

Read the [Grave Doxastikon](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2730_Glory.pdf), circling phthorae first.

### Day 226 (Thursday)

Read the [First Mode Doxastikon](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2130_Glory.pdf), circling phthorae first.

### Day 227 (Friday)

Drone drill. Sing Di–Ke–Zo–Ni' diatonic, then the same notes soft chromatic, then back. Ten times.

### Day 228 (Saturday)

Cold check: pick any Doxastikon on the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html), and before singing, list the phthorae and what each one does. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
