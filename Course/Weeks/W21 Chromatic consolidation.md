# Week 21 · Chromatic consolidation

Block 3 · The chromatic modes

**Goal:** Switch between the two chromatic modes and back to diatonic without losing your intonation.

## Days

### Day 121 (Monday)

Ear quiz: 10 Hard chromatic, then 10 Soft chromatic, then 10 diatonic on Pa. Log your three scores.

### Day 122 (Tuesday)

Read the [Plagal Second Anavathmoi](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3620_Ascent.pdf), first antiphon.

### Day 123 (Wednesday)

Read the [Second Mode Anavathmoi](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3220_Ascent.pdf), first antiphon.

### Day 124 (Thursday)

Read the [Plagal Second Lauds](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3680_Lauds.pdf), first sticheron.

### Day 125 (Friday)

Read the [Second Mode Lauds](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3280_Lauds.pdf), first sticheron.

### Day 126 (Saturday)

Block 3 check. Find this week's Sunday Apolytikion on the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html), whatever mode it's in, and sight-read it cold. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
