# Week 1 · Steps, the tie, petasti, kentimata

Block 1 · Reading mechanics

**Goal:** Make one-step motion automatic: ison, oligon, apostrophos, petasti, kentimata, and the yfen tie, all in two-beat meter.

Start with the workbook's intro track on [[Ex 001]]. You already chant, so this week is a calibration: the aim is clean parallagi at an even beat, with no guessing.

## Days

### Day 1 (Monday)

Workbook [[Ex 001]], [[Ex 002]], [[Ex 003]]. Sing each one in parallagi with no audio first, keeping a steady beat with your hand. Then play the recording and fix what you missed. If there's a commentary track, play it once.
