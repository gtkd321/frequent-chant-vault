# Week 34 · Grave Mode: heirmologic

Block 4 · All eight modes, two tempos

**Goal:** Read Grave Mode (Ἦχος Βαρύς) at heirmologic tempo, using Orthros material, and sight-read the matching Greek Sunday hymns.

**Mode:** [[Grave Mode]] (Ἦχος Βαρύς), base Ga or low Zo, enharmonic (on Ga) / diatonic (on low Zo). Intonation: Ἀανές (Aanes).

## Days

### Day 199 (Monday)

Review [[Grave Mode]], then sing 8 formulae from the [heirmologic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula7h.pdf) in parallagi.

### Day 200 (Tuesday)

Read [God is the Lord + Apolytikion](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3700_God_Is_Lord.pdf) and the [Apolytikion](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3705_Apolytikion.pdf): parallagi, then melos.

### Day 201 (Wednesday)

Read the [Kathismata](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3710_Kathismata.pdf): one kathisma.

### Day 202 (Thursday)

Read the [More Honorable (Τὴν τιμιωτέραν)](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3740_More.pdf).

### Day 203 (Friday)

Formula hunt: mark every cadence in the [Brief Lauds](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3790_Brief_Lauds.pdf) and name its formula. Then sing it.

### Day 204 (Saturday)

Cold Greek read. Open the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html), find a heirmologic hymn in this mode (an Apolytikion, a Katavasia or a heirmos), and sight-read it. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
