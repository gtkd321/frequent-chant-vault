# Week 35 · Grave Mode: sticheraric

Block 4 · All eight modes, two tempos

**Goal:** Read Grave Mode at sticheraric tempo, using Vespers material.

**Mode:** [[Grave Mode]] (Ἦχος Βαρύς), base Ga or low Zo, enharmonic (on Ga) / diatonic (on low Zo). Intonation: Ἀανές (Aanes).

## Days

### Day 205 (Monday)

Sing 8 formulae from [Grave Mode sticheraric formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula7.pdf).

### Day 206 (Tuesday)

Read [Lord, I have cried](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2700_Lord_I_have_cried.pdf) in parallagi, then melos.

### Day 207 (Wednesday)

Read the [stichera](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2720_Stichera.pdf): one sticheron.

### Day 208 (Thursday)

Read the [Dogmatikon](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2740_Dogmatikon.pdf), first half.

### Day 209 (Friday)

Read the [Aposticha](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2750_Aposticha.pdf). Mark the cadences first, then sing.

### Day 210 (Saturday)

Cold Greek read. On the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html), find a sticheron in this mode (Saturday Vespers or Lauds) and sight-read it. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
