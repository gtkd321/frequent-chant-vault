# Week 47 · Cold reading: Plagal First Mode, Plagal Second Mode, Grave Mode, Plagal Fourth Mode

Block 7 · Capstone: read any score

**Goal:** Sight-read unseen Greek scores at performance pace, with no audio until afterward.

## Days

### Day 277 (Monday)

Unseen Plagal First Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 278 (Tuesday)

Unseen Plagal Second Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 279 (Wednesday)

Unseen Grave Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 280 (Thursday)

Unseen Plagal Fourth Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 281 (Friday)

Redo the worst of this week's four. Mark the cadences and name the formulae first, then sing it again.

### Day 282 (Saturday)

Write three sentences in the log: what made you stop, what you misread, and what you'd drill. Rate yourself 1–5.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
