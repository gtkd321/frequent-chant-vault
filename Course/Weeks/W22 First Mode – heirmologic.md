# Week 22 · First Mode: heirmologic

Block 4 · All eight modes, two tempos

**Goal:** Read First Mode (Ἦχος Πρῶτος) at heirmologic tempo, using Orthros material, and sight-read the matching Greek Sunday hymns.

**Mode:** [[First Mode]] (Ἦχος Πρῶτος), base Pa, diatonic. Intonation: Ἀνανές (Ananes).

## Days

### Day 127 (Monday)

Review [[First Mode]], then sing 8 formulae from the [heirmologic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula1h.pdf) in parallagi.

### Day 128 (Tuesday)

Read [God is the Lord + Apolytikion](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3100_God_Is_Lord.pdf) and the [Apolytikion](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3105_Apolytikion.pdf): parallagi, then melos.

### Day 129 (Wednesday)

Read the [Kathismata](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3110_Kathismata.pdf): one kathisma.

### Day 130 (Thursday)

Read the [More Honorable (Τὴν τιμιωτέραν)](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3140_More.pdf).

### Day 131 (Friday)

Formula hunt: mark every cadence in the [Brief Lauds](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3190_Brief_Lauds.pdf) and name its formula. Then sing it.

### Day 132 (Saturday)

Cold Greek read. Open the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html), find a heirmologic hymn in this mode (an Apolytikion, a Katavasia or a heirmos), and sight-read it. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
