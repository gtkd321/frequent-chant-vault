# Week 8 · Changing the base: Pa, Ke, Vou, Ga

Block 1 · Reading mechanics

**Goal:** Read melodies that sit on Pa, Ke, Vou and Ga. This is the bridge from the scale of Ni into real modes.

On [[Ex 114]], try the lower-pitch recording too. Set the drone to match each base before you sing.
