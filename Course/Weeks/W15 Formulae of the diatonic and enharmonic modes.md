# Week 15 · Formulae of the diatonic and enharmonic modes

Block 2 · Hymns in six modes (workbook)

**Goal:** Move from reading note by note to reading in phrases. Learn the heirmologic formulae (theseis) for the six modes you've covered.

This is the most important shift of the year. Fluent readers don't decode neume by neume; they recognize stock phrases and cadences, the same way you read words rather than letters.

## Days

### Day 85 (Monday)

Read the [formulae workshop](https://music.samonastery.org/Workshop.pdf) introduction (it's short). Then read [Plagal Fourth heirmologic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula8h.pdf) and sing 6 of them in parallagi over the drone.

### Day 86 (Tuesday)

[First Mode heirmologic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula1h.pdf): sing 8 in parallagi. Find two of them in [[Ex 129]] or [[Ex 131]].

### Day 87 (Wednesday)

[Fourth Mode (Legetos) formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula4h.pdf): sing 8. Find two of them in [[Ex 137]]–[[Ex 143]].

### Day 88 (Thursday)

[Plagal First formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula5h.pdf): sing 8. Find two of them in [[Ex 144]]–[[Ex 148]].

### Day 89 (Friday)

[Third Mode formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula3h.pdf) and [Grave formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula7h.pdf): sing 5 from each.

### Day 90 (Saturday)

Phrase reading test. Open any workbook hymn you haven't looked at in two weeks. Mark the cadences in pencil (or in My Work), name the formula behind each one, then sing it through. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
