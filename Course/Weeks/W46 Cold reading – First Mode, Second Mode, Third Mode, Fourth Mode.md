# Week 46 · Cold reading: First Mode, Second Mode, Third Mode, Fourth Mode

Block 7 · Capstone: read any score

**Goal:** Sight-read unseen Greek scores at performance pace, with no audio until afterward.

## Days

### Day 271 (Monday)

Unseen First Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 272 (Tuesday)

Unseen Second Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 273 (Wednesday)

Unseen Third Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 274 (Thursday)

Unseen Fourth Mode piece from the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html): one heirmologic or sticheraric hymn you have never sung. Do one pass with no audio and record it, then check against the DCS recording or your teacher.

### Day 275 (Friday)

Redo the worst of this week's four. Mark the cadences and name the formulae first, then sing it again.

### Day 276 (Saturday)

Write three sentences in the log: what made you stop, what you misread, and what you'd drill. Rate yourself 1–5.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
