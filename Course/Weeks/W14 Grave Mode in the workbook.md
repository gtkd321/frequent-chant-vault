# Week 14 · Grave Mode in the workbook

Block 2 · Hymns in six modes (workbook)

**Goal:** Read the workbook's Grave Mode hymns (Ἦχος Βαρύς), with base Ga or low Zo and the enharmonic (on Ga) / diatonic (on low Zo) genus.

**Mode:** [[Grave Mode]] (Ἦχος Βαρύς), base Ga or low Zo, enharmonic (on Ga) / diatonic (on low Zo). Intonation: Ἀανές (Aanes).

Grave on Ga, as the workbook writes it. Exercises 159–161 are the Paschal Ἀνέστη Χριστός, Ἀνάστασιν Χριστοῦ and Ἀνέστης.
