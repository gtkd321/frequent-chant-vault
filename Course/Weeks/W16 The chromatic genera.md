# Week 16 · The chromatic genera

Block 3 · The chromatic modes

**Goal:** Hear and sing the hard chromatic (6–20–4) and soft chromatic (8–14–8) tetrachords, and recognize their phthorae.

**Mode:** [[Plagal Second Mode]] (Ἦχος Πλάγιος τοῦ Δευτέρου), base Pa, hard chromatic. Intonation: Νεχεανές (Necheanes).

## Days

### Day 91 (Monday)

Read [[Genera and Scales]], the chromatic sections. In the drone tool, choose Hard chromatic, base Pa. Tap Pa–Vou–Ga–Di slowly, then sing it ten times up and down.

### Day 92 (Tuesday)

Drone tool: Soft chromatic, base Di. Tap Di–Ke–Zo–Ni', then sing it. Alternate one hard pass and one soft pass so you feel the difference.

### Day 93 (Wednesday)

Read [[Phthorae and Chroai]], the chromatic phthorae. Do 10 cards in the ear quiz on Hard chromatic.

### Day 94 (Thursday)

Do 10 cards in the ear quiz on Soft chromatic. Then switch on the tuner and sing Di–Ke–Zo–Ni', checking each step lands within ±2 moria.

### Day 95 (Friday)

Look over the [Plagal Second heirmologic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula6h.pdf). Don't sing yet; just mark where the 20-moria step shows up.

### Day 96 (Saturday)

Cold check. Sing both tetrachords from memory with the tuner on, and log your average deviation. Rate yourself 1–5.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
