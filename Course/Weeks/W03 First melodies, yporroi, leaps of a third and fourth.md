# Week 3 · First melodies, yporroi, leaps of a third and fourth

Block 1 · Reading mechanics

**Goal:** Read the first church melodies, the yporroi, and leaps of a third and a fourth (kentima and elafron, alone and combined).

## Days

### Day 13 (Monday)

Workbook [[Ex 032]], [[Ex 033]]. Sing each one in parallagi with no audio first, keeping a steady beat with your hand. Then play the recording and fix what you missed. If there's a commentary track, play it once.

### Day 14 (Tuesday)

Workbook [[Ex 034]], [[Ex 035]], [[Ex 036]]. Sing each one in parallagi with no audio first, keeping a steady beat with your hand. Then play the recording and fix what you missed. If there's a commentary track, play it once.

### Day 15 (Wednesday)

Workbook [[Ex 037]], [[Ex 038]], [[Ex 039]]. Sing each one in parallagi with no audio first, keeping a steady beat with your hand. Then play the recording and fix what you missed. If there's a commentary track, play it once.

### Day 16 (Thursday)

Workbook [[Ex 040]], [[Ex 041]], [[Ex 042]], [[Ex 043]]. Sing each one in parallagi with no audio first, keeping a steady beat with your hand. Then play the recording and fix what you missed. If there's a commentary track, play it once.

### Day 17 (Friday)

Workbook [[Ex 044]], [[Ex 045]], [[Ex 046]], [[Ex 047]]. Sing each one in parallagi with no audio first, keeping a steady beat with your hand. Then play the recording and fix what you missed. If there's a commentary track, play it once.

### Day 18 (Saturday)

Cold check. Without any audio, sing [[Ex 039]], [[Ex 043]], [[Ex 047]] straight through in parallagi. Record yourself on your phone, then compare it with the workbook recording. Rate yourself 1–5 in the log.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
