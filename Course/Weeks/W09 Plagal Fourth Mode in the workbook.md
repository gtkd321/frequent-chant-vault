# Week 9 · Plagal Fourth Mode in the workbook

Block 2 · Hymns in six modes (workbook)

**Goal:** Read the workbook's Plagal Fourth Mode hymns (Ἦχος Πλάγιος τοῦ Τετάρτου), with base Ni and the diatonic genus.

**Mode:** [[Plagal Fourth Mode]] (Ἦχος Πλάγιος τοῦ Τετάρτου), base Ni, diatonic. Intonation: Νεάγιε (Neagie).

Plagal 4 sits on the natural scale of Ni you used all of Block 1, so it comes first.
