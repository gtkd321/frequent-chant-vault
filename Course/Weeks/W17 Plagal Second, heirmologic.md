# Week 17 · Plagal Second, heirmologic

Block 3 · The chromatic modes

**Goal:** Read short Plagal Second melodies. Hard chromatic on Pa.

**Mode:** [[Plagal Second Mode]] (Ἦχος Πλάγιος τοῦ Δευτέρου), base Pa, hard chromatic. Intonation: Νεχεανές (Necheanes).

## Days

### Day 97 (Monday)

[Plagal Second heirmologic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula6h.pdf): sing 6 in parallagi over the drone.

### Day 98 (Tuesday)

Read St Anthony's [Plagal Second, God is the Lord](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3600_God_Is_Lord.pdf). Use the site's Hear playback to check, or check with your teacher.

### Day 99 (Wednesday)

Read the [Plagal Second Apolytikion](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3605_Apolytikion.pdf) in parallagi, then sing the melos.

### Day 100 (Thursday)

Read the [Plagal Second Kathismata](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3610_Kathismata.pdf), first page only.

### Day 101 (Friday)

Find the Plagal Second Apolytikion (Ἀγγελικαὶ Δυνάμεις) in Greek on the [Digital Chant Stand](https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/dcs.html) or in your own Anastasimatarion. Sing it from the score, not from memory.

### Day 102 (Saturday)

Cold check: read the [Plagal Second Brief Lauds](https://music.samonastery.org/Orthros/b3690_Brief_Lauds.pdf). Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
