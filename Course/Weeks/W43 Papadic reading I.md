# Week 43 · Papadic reading I

Block 6 · Papadic tempo

**Goal:** Read slow, melismatic papadic melody: long phrases and the formulae inside them.

## Days

### Day 253 (Monday)

Read St Anthony's [papadic formulae page](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/papadic-formulae) intro. Then [First Mode papadic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula1p.pdf): sing 4.

### Day 254 (Tuesday)

[Fourth Mode Agia papadic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula4p.pdf): sing 4. Agia sits on Di.

### Day 255 (Wednesday)

[Plagal Fourth papadic formulae](https://music.samonastery.org/Formula8p.pdf): sing 4.

### Day 256 (Thursday)

Open the [Divine Liturgy in Byzantine notation](https://stanthonysmonastery.org/pages/divine-liturgy-byzantine-notation-modern) and pick a Cherubic Hymn. Read the first line only.

### Day 257 (Friday)

Same Cherubic Hymn, next two lines.

### Day 258 (Saturday)

Self-check: sing the opening of the Cherubic Hymn, record it, and log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
