# Week 39 · Chroai and attractions

Block 5 · Modulation and alteration

**Goal:** Hear the zygos, kliton and spathi colorings, and the attractions (ἕλξεις) on Zo, Vou and Ke.

## Days

### Day 229 (Monday)

Read the chroai section of [[Phthorae and Chroai]]. Find one example of each chroa in your own books (the Anastasimatarion or Doxastarion).

### Day 230 (Tuesday)

Attractions. In Plagal Fourth and First Mode melodies, mark every Zo that falls toward Ke, and every Vou pulled toward Pa. Sing them with the drone and tuner on.

### Day 231 (Wednesday)

Read the [Plagal Fourth Doxastikon](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2830_Glory.pdf) and mark the accidentals.

### Day 232 (Thursday)

Read the [Fourth Mode Doxastikon](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2430_Glory.pdf) and mark the accidentals.

### Day 233 (Friday)

Read the [Plagal First Doxastikon](https://music.samonastery.org/Vespers/b2530_Glory.pdf) and mark the accidentals.

### Day 234 (Saturday)

Cold check: any Doxastikon of the coming Sunday. Log a 1–5 rating.

### Sunday

Service day. Chant, listen, and write one line in your log. No new lesson.
